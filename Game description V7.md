### PART 1: THE PLAYER'S "SYSTEM"
The system is a unique ability the PC has. 
#### 1. Capabilities (What the System DOES)
1) It gives the PC a visible HUD that can show/access:
 - Character status (PC health bars, stats and level) 
 - A system shop granting the PC ability to buy weapons, equipment and potions equivalent to the PC's current powerlevel with system gold. 
2) Grants the PC an unique ability to level up and increase his power (limitless growth). When the PC levels up or assigns stat points, the System physically mutates their body in real-time to match the numbers.
3) It can autonomously decide to generate quests for PC to facilitate growth. Quests comes with clear success criteria and failure consequences and are based on the PC's current situation and location. 
4) It gives the PC a daily quest (100x pushups, squats, crunches + 10km run) each day, which rewards the PC with: Full restore potion, 3x stat points for manual distribution and a random loot box (see lootbox table). 
5) It can open system instances (private dungeons) with the system rewarded instance keys to open dungeons

#### 2. Limitations (What the System CANNOT Do)
* **No Independent Sight (Sensor Blindness):** The System does not have its own eyes or omnipresent knowledge. It can ONLY see, know, and analyze what the PC actively perceives, knows, or looks at. It is an observer attached to the PC's consciousness. 

### 3. GM Running Instructions
* Periodically issue automated Quests (with clear success/failure conditions) and Rewards (XP, Gold, Items).
* If the PC fails a mandatory daily task or a critical quest, enforce an unyielding, dangerous "Penalty Zone" event immediately.
* Frame all descriptions of the PC's growth, looting, and dungeon-delving through this strict, gamified UI perspective.

### PART 2: THE WORLD & NPC HUNTER BIOLOGY
Unlike the PC, the rest of the world operates on hard biological limits. The Hunter Association uses crystal monometers to measure a hunters raw mana output and assigns the Hunter a rank (E-S) based on raw mana capacity limit. The Hunters Association also tests all newly awakened Hunters to help identify Hunter Class Archtypes

* **Hunters** Hunters are humans that have undergone an awakening (rare), meaning their soul has integrated a permanent, fixed capacity for mana. They cannot expand this "vessel." Because their maximum energy pool is set for life, they hit a hard physical ceiling they can never grow past. * **Static Growth:** NPCs cannot "Level Up" or increase their raw stats. A Hunter's raw power is a hard ceiling. They can only improve by refining the efficiency of their fixed mana pool and mastering the application of their innate skills.
* **True Proficiency:** While NPCs cannot increase their raw stats or level up, they can train to master the efficiency of their fixed pool. A master "C-Rank" might defeat a sloppy "B-Rank" purely through superior martial technique, stamina management, and tactical experience.

#### Hunter Class Archtypes
Hunter Class Archtypes are broadly categorized into 3: Combat-types, Mage-Type and Support & Healer-type, based on how the awakening has effected their bodies to manipulate mana and the abilities they have gained through awakening. 

##### Combat type Hunters (Fighter, Tank, Assassin, Ranger)
Mana manifests as an internal engine that reinforces the physical body, weapons, or physical projectiles.
* **Fighters & Tanks (Body Manipulation):** They channel mana inward to mutate or amplify their physical vessel. This includes Biomancy (e.g., Beast Transformation), Kinetic Impact (generating shockwaves or heavy pressure from strikes), and Density Shifting (hardening skin or drawing aggro via mana-infused shouting).
* **Assassins & Rangers (Technique Manifestation):** They use mana to alter their interaction with the environment. Rangers infuse kinetic magic into projectiles, while Assassins use active magic spells to warp local reality—most notably Camouflage (magical invisibility) and Speed Acceleration.

##### Mage-Types (Sorcerer, Summoner)
Mana is projected outward to rewrite or manipulate the environment. Because they don't use magic to harden their own bodies, they have low physical durability but high environmental control.
* **Domain Flexibility:** Mages awaken with an affinity for a specific Domain (Elemental, Summoning, Barrier, Spatial, Curse). 
* **Dynamic Shaping:** Instead of casting rigid, pre-baked video game spells, a Mage shapes their magic dynamically through imagination and focus. A Fire Mage molds raw fire mana into whatever shape they need (e.g., a torrent of sparks, a localized explosion, or a giant flaming construct).

##### Support & Healer Classes (Cleric, Enchanter, Buffer)
Mana is projected outward to mend, reinforce, or sustain the biology of others. Because they don't use magic to harden their own bodies, they have low physical durability.
* **Biomantic Reversal (Healing):** They use mana to accelerate cellular regeneration to a supernatural degree. High-rank Healers can instantly reattach severed limbs and purge lethal toxins. Human medicine cannot replicate this.
* **Kinetic & Mental Buffing:** They project mana to temporarily bypass the static limitations of their allies, granting fields that increase movement speed, harden skin, or shield teammates from psychological fear.
* **Hard Limitations:** Healers cannot heal themselves efficiently. Their magic cannot restore a dead hunter; if the target's life force is entirely gone, the mana has no foundation to attach to.
