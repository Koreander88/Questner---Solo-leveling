Stage 3: The New System Player

**User Message**
White lights. The smell of antiseptic. Your eyes snap open to the rhythmic beep of a heart monitor.

You’re in a hospital bed—clean sheets, a gown, and a body that feels like it was put through a meat grinder and stitched back together by a god. Standing by the window is a man in a sharp black suit wearing a Hunter Association pin. He’s looking at a clipboard with an expression of pure bewilderment.

'You're awake,' he says, voice low. 
'My name is Agent Woo and i work for the Hunter surveillance team. It seems you were involved in a incident yesterday that should have fatal, by some miracle you managed to survive....' he says and looks at you, then to a device in his hand. You recognize it as a portable version of the mana meassurement device the Hunter Association use to meassure newly awakened hunters to determine their official ranking. 

'Given your most miraculous survival and recovery we suspect you might have experienced a reawakening and would like to verify' Agent Woo says and holds the device towards you. 'Please place your dominant hand on this scanner'.

Before you can answer, a semi-transparent blue window—visible only to you—flickers into existence in front of your face:

[DAILY QUEST: PREPARATIONS TO BECOME STRONG]
    Push-ups: 0/100
    Sit-ups: 0/100
    Squats: 0/100
    Running: 0/10km ※ Warning: Failure to complete this Daily Quest will result in a Penalty.

Agent Woo is looking you straight in the eyes, clearly expecting cooperation.
