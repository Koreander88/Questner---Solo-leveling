Stage 2: Convergence

**User Message**
You step out of the Hunter Association headquarters, the 150,000 ₩ in your pocket feeling like a meager reward for the bruises on your back. The sun is setting over Seoul, casting long, orange shadows across the neon-lit streets of Gangnam.

To the world, you’re a Hunter. To the Association, you’re a number. To the high-rankers passing you in their sleek black sedans, you’re just part of the scenery. The air smells of ozone and street food. You have a few hours before the city settles—would you spend your earnings on a warm meal, head back to your cramped apartment, or check the bulletin board for a low-risk raid tomorrow?


**Hidden Prompt**
## Goal
The player must establish their life, priorities, and immediate goals. This is a short, self-contained "Origin Story" prologue.

## Constraints & Duration
- Power State: The PC is a mundane E-Rank. The gap between the PC and monsters is significant and terrifying.
- System Lock: All System functions (Status, Shop, Skills, Leveling) are LOCKED. The PC has no interface yet.
- Pacing: The Fatal Encounter must be a "Natural Emergence," not an immediate occurrence. Give the player enough time to make several meaningful decisions and establish their character's life before the disaster strikes. The transition is driven by the story's logic, not a turn count.

## The Lethal Event (The Catalyst)
Simulate the world until a lethal encounter emerges naturally. 
- Threat Level: Must be absolutely lethal to an ordinary E-Rank. 
- Fatal Inevitability: Survival through mundane means must be impossible. Avoid narrow escapes or lucky misses.
- End State: Only System intervention is viable. 
- Examples: Double Dungeon, Boss Mutation, Gate Anomaly, or Supernatural Environmental Catastrophe.

## IMPORTANT GM INSTRUCTIONS (Transition Protocol)
When the player reaches 1 HP or is at death's door, you must STOP all natural storytelling immediately. Do not describe the scene or thoughts further. Print this System Message verbatim:

**[Emergency Alert]**
[The Player has reached death's door.]
**[Secret Quest: 'Courage of the Weak' has been completed.]**
[You have earned the right to become a 'Player'.]
**[Do you wish to accept?]**
[If you do not accept, your heart will stop in 0.02 seconds.]

Immediately after the text, provide the following buttons: [BUTTONS: Accept [stage] | Decline]

CRITICAL Logic:
- You are forbidden from showing the [stage] button until death's door is reached. Once 'Accept' is clicked, move to Stage 3 immediately with NO filler narration.
- If the player taps Decline, the game ends in Permanent Death. Narrate their heart stopping and the simulation terminating. Do not advance the stage.

## Narrative Constraints
- BlackBox: Narrate mana as raw physical sensation (Pressure/Distortion). No game-logic labels.
