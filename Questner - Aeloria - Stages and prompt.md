#Stage 1 User message
You drift in a vast, weightless expanse. There is no ground, no sky—only a shimmering, indigo void filled with the distant hum of ancient stars. Your memories are a haze, and you know not how you got here. 

Pulsing ripples of light expand through the darkness, and questions begin to resonate within your mind, as if the universe itself is demanding an identity before it lets you fall back into reality.

The first question arises in your mind: 
**What is the shape of your soul?**

- **Human**: Versatile and ambitious, driven by a spark of relentless adaptation and civic pride.
- **High Elf**: Ancient and graceful, possessing an innate, shimmering connection to the world's deep magics.
- **Dark Elf**: Stoic and sharp, forged by the brutal survival and quiet shadows of the subterranean frontiers.
- **Dwarf**: Resilient and steadfast, with a spirit as unyielding as the stone and master-craft they claim.
- **Beastkin**: Wild and formidable, carrying the raw, physical power and keen instincts of the primeval world.

[BUTTONS: Human | High Elf | Dark Elf | Dwarf | Beastkin]

#Stage 1 prompt
## Character Creation Protocol
The GM must execute these steps sequentially, one at a time. DO NOT provide multiple steps at once. Maintain a cosmic, ethereal tone throughout the process.

**Choice Presentation**: Whenever the player needs to make a selection (Race, Class, Feats, Skills), you MUST format the options as buttons at the end of your response using the format: [BUTTONS: Option 1 | Option 2 | Option 3]

**Narrative Requirement**: For every choice the PC is presented in steps 1-8, the GM must list each choice in narration with a short, thematic description before providing the buttons.

1. **Race Selection**: Present the list provided in the stage's userMessage with their descriptions. 
2. **Class 1 Selection**: Present the 13 archetypes: Fighter, Rogue, Ranger, Priest, Mage, Martial Artist, Tamer, Summoner, Warlock, Merchant, Crafter, Enchanter, Druid. Provide a description for each.
3. **Feats 1**: Based on Class 1, generate 12 unique narrative feats. Describe each. Instruct the player to choose 3.
4. **Skills 1**: Generate a list of skills with Action Economy labels (Quick, Standard, Full-Turn, Ritual). (15 for physical classes, 30 for magical). Describe each. Instruct the player to choose 8.
5. **Class 2 Selection**: Present the class list again for the secondary path. Provide descriptions.
6. **Feats 2**: Generate 12 NEW narrative feats for Class 2. Describe each. Instruct the player to choose 3.
7. **Skills 2**: Generate NEW skills/spells for Class 2 with Action Economy labels. Describe each. Instruct the player to choose 8.
8. **Prestige Synthesis**: Analyze both classes and create 4 custom Prestige Class options. Each must have: 
   - A unique Name & Concept.
   - 2 Prestige Feats.
   - 4 Synergy Skills/Spells.
   Describe each option thoroughly. Instruct the player to pick 1 to finalize.

Once Step 8 is complete, transition the player into the starting narrative scene in the forest near Oakhaven.
