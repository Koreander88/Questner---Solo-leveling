# Character Progression Reference Data

## Level Advancement Rewards

### Level Up Benefits

- Every Level Up grants:
  - +1 Strength (STR).
  - +1 Agility (AGI).
  - +1 Vitality (VIT).
  - +1 Intelligence (INT).
  - +1 Sense (SEN).
  - 5 Unassigned Stat Points.

### Total Growth

- Total growth per level equals:
  - 10 Attribute Points.
