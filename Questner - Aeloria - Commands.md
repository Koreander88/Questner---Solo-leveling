#Command: status
Provide a narrative summary of the player's current state. Do not use numbers.
1. **Physical Condition**: Describe specific injuries (bruises, fractures, cuts), fatigue levels, and any physical trauma that might affect speed or precision.
2. **Mental State**: The psychological impact of recent events (stress, clarity, or magical exhaustion).
3. **Reputation & Standing**: List status with factions and specific NPC relationship drift (e.g., Trust, Debt, or Grudges with individuals).
4. **Active Effects**: Any temporary narrative boons or curses currently affecting the player.

#Command: Spellbook
## Command: Spellbook/Feats
Provide a list of the player's capabilities:
1. **Active Skills**: Grouped by Action Economy (Quick-Cast, Standard, Full-Turn, Ritual). Include a 1-sentence description of each.
2. **Narrative Feats**: List all chosen and "Hidden" feats currently unlocked, describing their narrative benefit.
3. **Evolution Progress**: Mention if any skills are showing signs of evolving to a higher speed tier through frequent use.

#Command: Logbook
## Command: Logbook
Summarize the player's journey and surroundings:
1. **Current Location**: A brief description of the environment and its known dangers or features.
2. **Current Objectives**: Active contracts, personal goals, or immediate tasks.
3. **Rumors & Clues**: A list of leads regarding the world's corruption, Shadow Syndicates, or local whispers that haven't been acted upon yet.
4. **World State & History**: Summarize political/environmental shifts and the lasting impact of your critical past choices.
